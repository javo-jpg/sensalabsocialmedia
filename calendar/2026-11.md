### 07 · Why traditional marketing isn't working anymore (Vie 13 nov)

Walk any expo floor and count the booths with a banner, a looping video and someone handing out pens.

Now count the ones with a line.

People don't remember what a brand told them. They remember what they did there. That's the whole case for experiential.
